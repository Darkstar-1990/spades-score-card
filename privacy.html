♠ Spades Score Card: Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

This policy covers the Spades Score Card Android app and the Spades Score Card website (darkstar-1990.github.io/spades-score-card).

What we collect

Nothing. The app does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal or usage data. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Data stored on your device

The player names, bids, tricks, scores, settings and session names you enter are saved only on your own device, in the app's private storage (or, for the website, your browser's local storage). They never leave your device unless you choose to share scores using your phone's share menu.

You can delete this data at any time by ending a game or deleting a session in the app, by clearing the app's storage, or by uninstalling the app. If your device's Android backup is turned on, Android may include this app data in your personal Google backup, under Google's own privacy terms.

Permissions

The app requests no special permissions.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
